# Animated "The Starry Night" — Production Prompt Pack

## Primary Prompt (Image-to-Video / Text-to-Video)
Recreate Vincent van Gogh's *The Starry Night* (1889) as a highly accurate, 1:1 faithful reproduction. Preserve the original composition exactly: swirling night sky, crescent moon, stars, cypress tree in foreground, village below, and distant hills. Match the original post-impressionist style, oil paint texture, impasto depth, brush direction, and color palette with high fidelity.

Then animate the painting subtly so it appears to come alive while remaining faithful to the original artwork:
- the swirling sky moves slowly in continuous circular flow
- stars gently pulse and twinkle with warm golden glow
- the moon emits a soft radiating animated halo
- the cypress tree sways very slightly as if moved by light wind
- village window lights flicker warmly and naturally
- cloud-like paint swirls drift smoothly across the sky

Keep motion calm, atmospheric, and seamless. Maintain the exact visual identity of the original painting: no added objects, no composition changes, no character insertion, no modern artifacts, no camera shake. Preserve visible oil brush strokes and canvas-like texture throughout.

Output target: cinematic, dreamy, calm, slightly surreal night atmosphere, seamless looping animation, ultra-high-detail 4K look.

## Concise Generator Prompt
Faithful 1:1 reproduction of Vincent van Gogh's *The Starry Night*, preserving exact composition, color harmony, oil impasto texture, and brushstroke character; subtle living-painting animation only: slow swirling sky flow, softly pulsating stars, glowing crescent moon halo, slight cypress sway, warm village light flicker, gentle drifting cloud swirls; calm dreamy atmosphere, post-impressionist integrity preserved, seamless loop, cinematic framing, high-resolution 4K quality.

## Negative Prompt
Do not alter composition. Do not add or remove objects. No new buildings, people, animals, text, logos, borders, or modern elements. No hard cuts, jitter, stutter, warping, melting artifacts, excessive morphing, oversaturation, neon colors, blur loss, or photorealistic conversion. No style drift away from post-impressionist oil painting texture.

## Looping & Motion Guidance
- Loop length: 6-10 seconds.
- Frame rate: 24 fps or 30 fps.
- Motion amplitude: very low to low (subtle movement only).
- Use sinusoidal or ease-in/ease-out flow for loop closure.
- Ensure first and last frames are near-identical for seamless looping.
- Keep foreground village stable; most movement in sky and light effects.

## Optional Shot/Camera Constraints
- Locked camera (no pan, tilt, zoom, dolly, or handheld movement).
- Preserve original framing and aspect ratio unless output format requires minimal padding.
- If upscaling, keep brushstroke edges crisp and painterly.
